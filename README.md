## Hi there, I'm Nurislam 👋

# 💫 About Me:
Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Lafayette College (B.S. ECE, Minor in CS, 4.00 GPA, Dean's List, Oechsle Scholar) focused on embedded systems, robotics, and control engineering.<br> <br>As a Bergh Family Fellow & EXCEL Scholar, I research precise motion control of a 6-DOF robotic arm. I replaced the stock firmware on an AR4 MK3 with a custom MATLAB/Simulink control stack: six real-time joint controllers with S-curve velocity profiles, a staged homing sequencer, and closed-form inverse kinematics validated on hardware to 2–4 mm end-effector error, deployed to a Teensy 4.1 via Embedded Coder. On top of that I built an HSV vision pipeline for colour-sorting pick-and-place. I also built a microcontroller-free collision-avoidance robot with 555 timers, comparators, and differential amplifiers, and a bare-metal STM32 PID motor controller in register-level Embedded C with no HAL.<br> <br>I also build at hackathons: 1st place in the Environmental Track at MakeCU 2026 (1 of 31 teams, where I built the radar hardware and 3D-printed enclosure for NextUp), Best Quantum Debut at YQuantum 2026, and 2nd Most Innovative Project at the Lehigh Hackathon 2026.<br> <br>Beyond engineering, I am Co-President of the Muslim Student Association and a Resident Advisor. I am always seeking opportunities to build, learn, and collaborate across hardware and software.<br>

# 🔧 Featured Projects:

- 🤖 **[AR4 Custom Control System](https://github.com/nurislamabd/AR4-Custom-Control-System)** — Custom MATLAB/Simulink control stack replacing stock firmware on an AR4 MK3 6-DOF arm; six real-time joint controllers with S-curve profiling and backlash compensation, staged homing sequencer, and closed-form IK (2–4 mm end-effector error across 5 poses) generating C for a Teensy 4.1, with an HSV vision pipeline for colour-sorting pick-and-place
- 🚿 **[NextUp](https://github.com/goraxyy/nextup)** *(MakeCU 2026, 1st place Environmental Track, team of 4)* — Shower-stall availability system for shared dorm bathrooms. I owned the hardware: an HLK-LD2410C mmWave radar on a Raspberry Pi with presence detection and empty-stall auto-calibration, plus a 3D-printed enclosure for the Pi, display, and power bank
- 🔩 **[STM32 PID Motor Controller](https://github.com/nurislamabd/stm32-pid-motor-controller)** — Bare-metal closed-loop PID velocity controller in register-level Embedded C (no HAL) on an STM32F446RE; PWM motor drive, hardware quadrature encoder decoding, validated across two independent step-response trials (30 ms rise time, <7% steady-state error)
- 🏎️ **[RaceBall](https://github.com/raceball/raceball)** *(HackPSU Fall 2026, team of 3)* — Multiplayer racing game steered by phone tilt, with a shared big screen. I owned art direction, audio, and UI, and wrote an adaptive render-quality controller and the drift and heading-clamp gameplay rules
- 🦾 **[AR4 ROS2 Joint Controller](https://github.com/nurislamabd/ar4-ros2-joint-controller)** — ROS2 C++ node publishing 6-DOF joint states at 50 Hz through physics-based trapezoidal motion profiles, visualized live in RViz via URDF/TF (simulation)
- ⚙️ **[C++ Motor Simulator](https://github.com/nurislamabd/cpp-motor-simulator)** — Physics-based trapezoidal motion simulator for robotic joint control, validated against MATLAB Simulink to <0.5% positional error
- 🚦 **[Traffic Light FSM Controller](https://github.com/nurislamabd/traffic-light-fsm)** — Moore FSM in SystemVerilog on a Nexys A7 FPGA for a two-intersection controller with pedestrian priority, validated in Vivado simulation and on hardware
- ⚛️ **[QuBots — Quantum Portfolio Optimization](https://github.com/goraxyy/qubots)** *(collaborative, YQuantum 2026)* — Portfolio optimization formulated as a QUBO and solved via QAOA on an 8-qubit neutral-atom simulation; won Best Quantum Debut
